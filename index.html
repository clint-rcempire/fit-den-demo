About FIT DEN

More than a place
to work out.

Welcome to FIT DEN — Your Fitness, Your Den. Located in Amghata, FIT DEN offers a dedicated space for fitness enthusiasts, beginners and strength-focused training. From cardio and general gym workouts to strength training and powerlifting, discover a training environment built around your fitness journey. Your fitness. Your goals. Your den.

Spacious Floor

Big, bright training hall

Large, bright, spacious hall with light wood-look floor and big arched mirrors — cramped basement gym jaisa nahi.

Modern Equipment

Never wait for a machine

Plate-loaded leg press / hack squat, cable crossover, Smith machine, KAIZEN machine, bumper plates, dumbbell racks and cardio area.

Community

Supportive atmosphere

Members say the trainer and members are supportive, the gym is well organised, and it is good for beginners. FIT DEN 1st Anniversary Fitness Competition bhi hua tha.
